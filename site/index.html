03 · Exploration log

It started on the ground, with how buildings actually run.

I joined Facilio in January 2022 in a data-driven building operations internship and moved into full-time implementation that August.

Since then I have been the person a client works with from the first discovery workshop until the system is live and stable: enterprise and mid-market rollouts, owning scope, timelines, risks and dependencies as the primary client-facing lead.

In January 2026 my role widened to automation. I co-built the AI-assisted workspace our implementation team now uses to run projects, and I build AI agent workflows that take repetitive work out of delivery.

How I solve problems

  1. Requirements first

    Discovery workshops and BRDs turn how a client operates into something the platform can be configured against.

  2. Design before configuration

    Modules, workflows, roles and integrations are designed, and their risks named, before anything is built.

  3. Test against what was asked

    UAT closes the loop: scenarios trace back to requirements, defects are triaged, sign-off is formal.

  4. Stay through hypercare

    A go-live is a sign-off, not a date. Issues after launch are root-caused and fed back to product.
